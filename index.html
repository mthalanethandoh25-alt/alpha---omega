About Alpha & Omega

Alpha & Omega is a faith-sharing space created to encourage people through Scripture, prayer and reflection.

“I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End.” - Revelation 22:13

Created by Thando to share God's Word, to pray together and to encourage everyone in Underberg and beyond.
